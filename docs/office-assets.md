## Attribution notes

CC BY requires credit, a license link, and notice of changes. These images were center-cropped to 1920 × 1080 and recompressed as WebP. Make these credits available with the product wherever the bundled images are used.

The Commons page for the Sydney photo names Daniel Falconer in its summary, while its Flickr-derived structured data identifies the photographer as J c (`catsper`). Both names are recorded in [office-assets.json](./office-assets.json) to preserve the Commons provenance discrepancy.

The Dubai view includes Downtown Dubai's fountain promenade and small people in the foreground; its skyline remains the main subject. It is the only frame with a noticeable public crowd and the most tourism-oriented foreground. The Sydney frame includes the Harbour Bridge and Luna Park along with the CBD.
