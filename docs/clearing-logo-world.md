## File checks

The nine downloaded SVGs parsed as XML. Tencent PNG signature and dimensions (2160 × 290) were checked after extraction. The downloaded files are mirrored under `assets/companies/logos/` and `apps/web/public/assets/companies/logos/`; each pair was written from the same response bytes. Tencent mirror files are byte-identical. Checks found no `<script>`, `<foreignObject>`, external `href`/`src`, or external CSS URL.

Machine-readable source metadata, company decisions, and local paths are in [`clearing-logo-world.json`](clearing-logo-world.json).

## Integration status — 2026-10-07

The 10 eligible downloaded candidates were integrated into the current catalog after this research pass. Preparation counts in this report remain historical; the live audit is [LOGO_AUDIT.md](LOGO_AUDIT.md), with 47 local real logos and 44 monograms overall after the final old-Telstra removal.
